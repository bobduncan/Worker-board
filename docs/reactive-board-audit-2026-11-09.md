# Plan tab audit: actions against the principles (2026-11-09)

Read-only. Principles: PRINCIPLES.md 1-9, and the person's U1-U6 (U1 always reactive, U2 queue don't call, U3 little text,
U4 colour only for what needs the person, U5 every ask has its button and honest links, U6 = principle 2 time limit, retry, stuck).

Short names used in the table:
- **T22 line** = the wait line (src/waitLine.mjs, drawn by `drawWaitLine`, board.js:218): Sent / Picked up / Done / Late. Its Late is only
  rule 8's "not delivered" (src/supervisor/rules/watch.mjs:462-477): once a word reached the chat there is no time limit any more.
- **Tracked words** = the commands `isPersonWord` gives a T22 line (waitLine.mjs:276-287): answers, `how-go`, `goal-ask`, `talk`,
  `vibe-not-yet`, `how-not-yet`, `recheck`, the six `brainstorm*` words, `refine`, recap feedback, stop/remove. Every other word has no line.
- **Note** = a one-off line under the head (`focusUi.note`, `visionUi.note`, `brainstormUi.note`) that stays until the next click; **toast** =
  the Goals line that goes after 8 s (`goalsSay`, board.js:13126, GOAL_NOTE_MS 8000).
- **Words** = the server puts a word in the outbox (server.mjs `toPartner` 1474, or the page's own `send()` to /api/say, board.js:22940) that
  the Workers mod types into the chat. Nothing in the work store.

## Top 10 mismatches (by how often the person meets them)

1. **Talk card says "Your partner is thinking…" while the line under it says the word waits to be picked up, or was answered long ago.** Fix: drop the guessed text when a T22 line stands, and draw the line's state ("waits for X", "X is on it since 14:02", "answered 14:05 without a question") as the card's one status.
2. **Brainstorm answer shows "thinking · usually under a minute" for up to 30 min, beside a wait line that can say something else.** Fix: same change on `brainstormPartnerView`: read the word's T22 line (`threadWaitView`) instead of guessing from the queue.
3. **Every Vision Confirm, mark or "Yes, mine" waits for a partner turn, even though it only records the person's decision.** Fix: the server writes the decision into the plan page itself (as goals.md moves already do) and tells the partner afterwards; the slide then leaves because the file says so, not page memory.
4. **"Make it my focus" on Vision step 5 always fails with "Not a move of the Vision tab".** Fix: send it to /api/goals (the focus way), or add `focus` to the plan route's way to `focusNow`.
5. **After a word reached the chat, nothing ever turns late: "X is on it" or "waits for its turn" can stand for hours.** Fix: give the T22 line a second limit after delivery (e.g. 30 min with no result: Late, with Send again also for a running chat).
6. **Many clicks get one note or an 8-second toast and then nothing: This is the goal definition, Pick, Did we reach the goal, Ask for a Done when, Ask again for the demo, Undo, Mark done.** Fix: add these word commands (`focus`, `vibe-agreed`, `goal-recheck`, `done-when`, `goal-demo`, `undo`, `vibe-there-no`) to the tracked list and draw their line where the button was.
7. **"Your partner writes its Done when" shows in amber, from page memory, and vanishes on reload; on the Goals part there is not even that.** Fix: muted colour, and read it from the word's T22 line (or the store), not `focusUi.asked`.
8. **A talk or brainstorm answer is written, but its word to the partner is a second call from the page; when that fails, the person is told to go to the Brainstorm part and answer again.** Fix: one server action that writes the thread and queues the word together (as `talkBar` already does), with Send again in the line.
9. **Vision "preparing" spinners start the moment a word is delivered, and "Send it again from its box" points at a box that was emptied.** Fix: use the T22 line (Picked up only on the chat's turn on that word), and keep the person's words for Send again.
10. **Texts name the main chat for things the board does itself (Create: "the main chat is asked for N steps"; Go: "the main chat hands them out"), and "the main chat is asked to start it" shows in orange.** Fix: say what the board did ("3 tasks made: B12, B13, B14"; "the board hands them out"), and draw plain information muted.

## Patterns

1. **The partner writes what code could write.** The Vision tab is words only ("the partner is the page's only writer"), so even a Confirm or a mark waits for a chat turn; the same for Make a step from this (Talk to Main) and the effort switch (the main chat). Principle 1 and 7, U2.
2. **Three wait systems, and two of them guess.** The T22 line (outbox + the mod's turn report), the Vision working lines (delivered = "preparing", plan.mjs) and the talk/brainstorm cards ("thinking" when nothing says otherwise) sit on the same card and can disagree. Principles 3, 4 and 9, U1.
3. **The wait line covers a fixed list of commands.** Every other word a click sends gets a one-off note, an 8-s toast or page memory ("asked"), and nothing after. U1, U6.
4. **Time limits stop at delivery.** Late is rule 8's "not delivered" only; once a word is in the chat there is no limit, no retry, and Send again only for a chat that does not run. Principle 2, U6.
5. **The page runs two steps itself.** It writes the thread or ask, then types the word in a second call; a failure in between leaves half an action that the person mends by hand, often on another sub-page. Old texts still name the main chat for work code does now. Principles 1 and 5, U3, U5.
