# The protocol: the words the board and the chats share

The board reads what the chats write and types into their chats, and the chats read what it types. These are the
words both sides keep to. A chat that writes them otherwise is read wrongly by the board, so the job texts in this
folder point here. `<main>` is the main chat's title from the list of chats, `<name>` a chat's title, `HH:MM` a
time. Every name, path and word of a project comes from its settings or its own files, never from here.

## What a chat writes, and the board reads

Quality routing is initially observe-only (`../docs/model-routing.md`). Existing mod commands remain the supported
chat workflow. New managed server commands fail closed without capability proof; they are not exposed by older
mods. A note or requested model is not a native setting acknowledgement or a candidate approval.

B301: the board reads no task, no end of a task and no question from a chat's words. A step comes from the work store
and ends with `work report`; what the person must answer is an ask of the store (`work ask`). The few words below are
read only for what the store does not hold.

## The work store

The work stands in one record per item, written only by the board. A chat works it with the Workers mod's `work`
tool; the board checks every call against the flow file and refuses, in one line, what the flow does not allow. The
tool answers with the record: work from it, not from a message.

- **An item** is `<work>/<id>.md`: a head of `Key: value` lines up to the first blank line (`Id`, `Kind`, `Title`,
  `State`, `Owner`, `Pinned`, `Activity`, `Goal`, `Blocked by`, `Waits for`, `Since`, `Estimate`, `Branch`,
  ...), then `## ` sections (`Summary`, `What we build`, `Context`, `History`, ...). Steps are `B<n>`, asks for the
  person `Q<n>`, instructions `I<n>`, notes `N<n>`; the board gives the ids, and an id never comes back.
- **The flow file** (`<work>/flow.json`, the default `examples/work/flow.json`) holds the states of a step and who moves
  it from one to the next, and when. It, not a job text, says who moves an item and when; the person may change it.
- **History**: every change writes one line, `- <date time> <who>: <what>`.
- **The board hands out all work** (2026-10-25, the redesign's Part 2): one list of the steps that are ready (refined,
  `Refined: ready`, nothing they are blocked by or wait for open, no blocking ask and no answer left to fold in, their
  kind's standard not holding them), in one order: `Priority: fix` first, then the goal map's focus goal's, then the
  rest, each by id. The moment a worker's Workers mod reports it idle (and only then: no chat read by the board's older
  inference, unknown, off or suspended), the board gives it the first suitable one: its pin first; then in the list's
  order, a follow-up (a step that waits on one that worker finished, or a result check's fix of a step it built) to
  that worker when it is idle at the give (`followUpMinutes` 0, a preference only; above 0 it is kept for it that long),
  else to a worker on the step's model, else to the next; never one whose Touches overlap a step given or under way. The give is
  the flow's server move refined → queued ("the board hands it out"): Owner the worker, History "refined → queued
  (given by the board to <worker>)". A step not taken within two minutes of its line goes back (queued → refined,
  History "queued → refined (taken back from <worker>: not taken within 2 min)", with "; stuck" when the worker sat
  idle through it: its lane shows "Stuck" until it reports anew). A worker that went busy after its line is waited for,
  and its step goes back, not stuck, once it reports idle without having taken it. A step whose line has not gone
  `heldGiveMinutes` (20) after the give (held for the worker's model, a clear, a mod restart, or its chat off) goes back,
  not stuck ("its line was held"), and goes to another worker for that long. A step given to a chat missing from the
  list goes back on the same clock (from its line, else from the give). A give the work API refuses is not tried again
  for that worker for 15 minutes, and the ready list says why. With ready steps waiting, no worker idle and fewer workers running than the setting `maxWorkers`,
  the board asks the main chat once for one more (`start "<chat>"` for a worker of the list whose chat is off, else
  `spawn workers`), counting only the steps a new worker could take (no Touches overlap) less the workers asked for in
  the last 10 minutes that have not reported yet. Nothing is given, taken back or asked for while the person pauses or
  resumes, or a limit is full; after either the take-back clock starts anew.
- **The person's pin** (`Pinned: <worker>`): the person pins a new or refined step to a worker on the board ("Pin
  to", "Move to", "Unpin"); the board gives it to that worker only. A pin to a chat gone from the list for 30 minutes
  the board takes off. Steps queued before the redesign went back on the list at its rollout ("back on the ready list:
  the board hands out all tasks now"), a placement the person had made becoming a pin.
- **Fix first**: a step with `Priority: fix` in its head is a fix for the worker board (a bug the person reports in
  the board or in a chat's tools for it). It stands first on the board's list: the board gives it to the first free
  worker, `work next` lists it first among steps of the same state ("B<id> queued, board fix: ..."), the lanes' queues
  and the Backlog tab show it first with a "fix" chip, and the Focus page says of a part that waits for it "A board fix
  goes first: B<id>". The main chat, the partner and the person mark it, with `priority` fix on `work new`. An
  incident's fix is one as any: no queue of its own.

mod clears a worker after the turn in which it reported its step, even when more work follows: first `save-log`

and a chat with background work still running), then the mark on the card, then /clear once the chat is idle (a
prompt or turn that comes in first stops it: a real turn on the same session drops the clear, and a /clear of the mod
runs only as the one of the clear still on its way). It is compacted instead, its card saying why (`clearNote`), when
the board does not answer that it is a worker (`role` in turn-ended's answer), the person pauses, its answer asks the

saved. A turn-ended answered late (the board checks a report then) is waited for apart, up to 15 s, asked again with
